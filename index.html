About

My background sits at the point where technology and people meet. At BITUNIX, a cryptocurrency exchange, I worked omnichannel support queues across live chat and Zendesk before moving into the retention team, where I engaged users directly through Discord. Crypto support is unforgiving: the stakes are real, the users are stressed, and there's no room for a vague answer.

That experience taught me how to translate technical account issues into clear, calm guidance, and how to build the kind of trust that keeps a user engaged rather than lost. At Pa-cent Technologies, I brought that same clarity to teaching non-technical learners everyday software, helping them build real confidence rather than just complete a session.
